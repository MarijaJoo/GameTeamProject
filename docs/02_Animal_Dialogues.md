# Animal Dialogues

### Overview

This document contains all dialogue used by the animal mentor throughout the game. The dialogue is written in simple, friendly language that is easy for children to understand. The mentor gives immediate feedback after each decision, helping the player learn safe online behavior.

---

# Welcome Message

- Hello, friend!

I'm your cyber safety buddy! Together we'll learn how to stay safe while using the internet. Let's begin!

---

# Scenario 1 – Suspicious Link

### Correct Choice

- Great job!

Unknown links can hide viruses or tricks. It's always safer to delete suspicious messages.

### Wrong Choice

- Be careful!

Not every message is safe. Some links can harm your computer.

---

# Scenario 2 – Strong Password

### Correct Choice

- Excellent!

Strong passwords help keep your account safe.

### Wrong Choice

- Let's try again!

Easy passwords are simple for hackers to guess.

---

# Scenario 3 – Personal Information

### Correct Choice

- Well done!

Your personal information should stay private.

### Wrong Choice

- Oops!

Never tell strangers your address or other private information.

# Scenario 4 – Strange Link from a Friend

### Correct Choice

- Smart thinking!

Sometimes a friend's account can be hacked. Always ask first.

### Wrong Choice

- Careful!

It's better to check before opening unusual links.
